How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded file GateCall.apk.
  3. If your phone asks, tap Settings and allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Gate Call, enter your cellphone number and the code we SMS you.
  5. Tap Scan my gate sign and scan the QR code on your gate sign.

Already have Gate Call?

Download it again and install it over the old one. You keep your account, gate signs and settings.
