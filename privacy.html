Privacy notice (POPIA)

Redbear Group (Pty) Ltd, trading as Gate Call (registration number 2017/383403/07) is the responsible party for your personal information. We take your privacy seriously and follow the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

From you: your cellphone number, your name and address (if you give them), your backup contact, your gate details, your settings and your payment status. We do not store full card numbers.
From visitors at your gate: what they type (name, reason, optional cellphone number and photo), call details (time, length, result), a random identifier for their phone's browser, their IP address and browser type, and call recordings if you turn recording on.
From your phone: a notification token so we can alert you.

Why we use it

To connect calls, send you alerts, show your visits, recognise repeat callers and spam, collect payment, support you, protect the service from abuse, and meet the law. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

Only with companies that help us run Gate Call, such as hosting, calling, SMS, push notification and payment providers (for example PayFast), and where the law requires. Some of these providers may store information outside South Africa. We require them to protect it.

How long we keep it

Recordings: for the days you choose. Visitor messages and call history: while your account is active. Caller identifiers and IP addresses: no longer than 12 months for blocked callers and 30 days for others. Your account information: until you cancel, then it is deleted within 30 days, except records the law makes us keep.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email info@camedia.online. If you are not happy you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
